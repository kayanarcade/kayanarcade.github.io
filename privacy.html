Plushie Pile Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

This policy covers Plushie Pile, the Android game published by Kayan Arcade on Google Play (package com.kayanarcade.plushiepile). It explains what data the game uses, who receives it, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Your progress is saved only on your phone. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and we receive none of your data.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google's ad software collects some device data to show and measure those ads.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. What the game stores on your phone

Plushie Pile saves your game progress in a file on your device: the levels you have played and the stars you earned, your coins, boosters, collected plushies and skins, what you have bought in the shop, and your sound and music settings. This file never leaves your phone, and we cannot see it.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and it does not use its own analytics or tracking.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad during levels and offers optional "watch an ad" rewards. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland). Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

To choose, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the game collects and sends to Google:

  • Device identifiers: your device's advertising ID and app set ID.
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address.
  • App interactions: for example app launches, ad views and taps.
  • Diagnostics: for example crash and performance information about the ad software.

This data is encrypted in transit. Google may use it to show personalised ads, subject to the consent and settings described below. For details, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Consent in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form before any ads load. You can choose whether Google may use your data for personalised ads. To change your answer later, tap Privacy choices at the bottom of the game's title screen.

Limiting ad personalisation anywhere

On most Android phones you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, under Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path varies by phone).

Removing ads

If you buy Remove ads in the game's shop, the game stops requesting ads immediately. From then on it no longer starts Google's ad software when it launches, so no ad data is collected.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Coin packs, the starter pack and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. The game only receives confirmation of what you bought so it can give you the item, and records it in your progress on your phone. Your purchases stay tied to your Google account, not to us.

4. Children

Plushie Pile is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the game and data was collected by the ad software, contact us and we will help you reach Google to have it removed.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your progress stays on your phone until you delete it. Tap Reset progress on the title screen, or uninstall the game, to remove it.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob is kept and handled by Google under its own policies. You can reset your advertising ID in your phone's settings, and manage your data at myaccount.google.com.
  • Records of purchases are kept by Google Play with your Google account.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Because we do not receive or hold any of your data, requests about ad data are best made to Google through the links above. You are also welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

8. Contact

Kayan Arcade. For questions about this policy or your data, open an issue on our GitHub page.
