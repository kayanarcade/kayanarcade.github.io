Plushie Pile Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 September 2026

This policy covers Plushie Pile, the Android game published by Kayan Arcade on Google Play (package com.kayanarcade.plushiepile). It explains what data the game uses, who receives it, and the choices you have.

In short

  • Plushie Pile is made for players of all ages, including children.
  • Your progress is saved only on your phone. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics, and we receive none of your data.
  • The game shows child-safe ads from Google AdMob. They are never personalised, and the game does not send your advertising ID. Google's ad software collects some device data to show and measure those ads.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. What the game stores on your phone

Plushie Pile saves your game progress in a file on your device: the levels you have played and the stars you earned, your coins, boosters, collected plushies and skins, what you have bought in the shop, and your sound and music settings. This file never leaves your phone, and we cannot see it.

The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location, and it does not use its own analytics or tracking.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows a banner ad during levels and offers optional "watch an ad" rewards. No ad is shown when the game starts, and full-screen ads play only when the player chooses to watch one. These ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC (Google Ireland Limited for users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland). AdMob is certified by Google for use in apps for children.

Child-directed ads for everyone

Because children play Plushie Pile, every ad request the game makes is marked as child-directed, for every player of every age. This means:

  • Ads are never personalised: no interest-based ads and no remarketing.
  • The game does not send your device's advertising ID. It does not even have permission to read it.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (rated G).

To show and measure these ads and to prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the game still collects and sends to Google:

  • An app set ID, a device identifier that can be used only by apps from the same developer. It is not the advertising ID.
  • Approximate location, worked out from your IP address.
  • App interactions: for example app launches, ad views and taps.
  • Diagnostics: for example crash and performance information about the ad software.

This data is encrypted in transit, and Google does not use it for personalised advertising. Google handles it under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing ads

If you buy Remove ads in the game's shop, the game stops requesting ads immediately. From then on it no longer starts Google's ad software when it launches, so no ad data is collected.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play)

Coin packs, the starter pack and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the payment and your payment details under the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service. The game only receives confirmation of what you bought so it can give you the item, and records it in your progress on your phone. Your purchases stay tied to your Google account, not to us.

4. Children and parents

Plushie Pile is made for players of all ages, including children under 13. It follows the Google Play Families Policy and is designed with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) in mind.

  • The game never asks a child for personal information: no name, email address, photos, contacts or precise location, and no account or sign-in.
  • There is no chat, no way to talk to other players, and no links out of the game other than the ads themselves.
  • Ads are child-directed and never personalised, as described in section 2. The game does not show Google's ad consent form, because every player is already treated as a child.
  • Things bought with the game's coins show a price in coins. Real-money items show Google Play's price in your own currency, and are always paid for on Google Play's own purchase screen. Parents can require approval for purchases with Google Play's parental controls or Google Family Link.

We do not receive or keep any data about your child. If you have a question or would like help asking Google to delete data its ad software collected, contact us (see section 8).

5. Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your progress stays on your phone until you delete it. Tap Reset progress on the title screen, or uninstall the game, to remove it.
  • Data collected by Google AdMob is kept and handled by Google under its own policies. You can manage your Google data at myaccount.google.com.
  • Records of purchases are kept by Google Play with your Google account.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data. Because we do not receive or hold any of your data, requests about ad data are best made to Google through the links above. You are also welcome to contact us and we will help where we can.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

8. Contact

Kayan Arcade. For questions about this policy or your data, open an issue on our GitHub page.
